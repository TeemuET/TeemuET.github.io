I am a Master's student majoring in Applied Mathematics at Aalto University, currently spending my Autumn 2026 semster studying abroad at Nanyang Technological University (NTU) Singapore. I previously completed my BSc in Science and Technology (major in Mathematics and System Sciences) at Aalto. My academic interests have mostly focused on applying mathematics to real-world problems in the form of optimization, statistical inference and machine learning. 

My previous professional and academic experience includes:

**Quantitative Analyst Trainee**<br>
*[Kuntarahoitus (MuniFin)](https://www.kuntarahoitus.fi/en/), a Finnish financial institution serving municipalities and public-sector organizations*<br>
*Risk Management & Analytics, Risk Models Team*
* I worked on developing and maintaining financial risk models (focusing on VaR, Expected Shortfall, and CSRBB) which involved building automated regulatory reporting and backtesting workflows. On the valuation side, I contributed to a new QuantLib-based pricing framework by resolving pricing and sensitivity discrepancies and data stream issues. To support daily operations, I refactored scripts for modularity and developed automated risk reports and alerts using Python, SQL, PowerShell and Power BI.  

**Research Assistant**<br>
*Aalto University, Department of Computer Science, Probabilistic Machine Learning Group*
* I built an experiment framework for **Calibrated Bayesian Optimization** with a focus on investigating the most relevant metrics for measuring the performance of calibrated BO. I also performed ablation studies and performance comparisons on existing literature and brainstormed ideas on combining sharpness with calibration. These early-stage investigations contributed to a co-authored preprint titled *Online Sharp-Calibrated Bayesian Optimization*, which was led by [Marshal Sinaga](https://marshalarijona.github.io/) and [Julien Martinelli](https://julienmartinelli.github.io/).

**Research Assistant**<br>
*Aalto University, Department of Applied Physics, Complex Systems and Materials Group*
* I developed **Gaussian Process Regression (GPR)** models to reconstruct 3D data in Atom Probe Tomography (APT). This research led to a first-authored paper, which was recently published in [*Microscopy and Microanalysis*](https://academic.oup.com/mam).

**Teaching Assistant**<br>
*Aalto University, Department of Mathematics and Systems Analysis*
  * Statistical Inference (MS-C1620)
  * Calculus 1 (MS-A0107)
  * A First Course in Probability and Statistics (MS-A0502)

**Projects & Awards**<br>
* **Winner, Sustainable Finance in the Quantum Era Hackathon (2024):** Built and pitched a portfolio optimization tool for retail investors using quantum computing and ESG scores.
